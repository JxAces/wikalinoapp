# Wikalino app icon

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. The golden open book represents reading, the emerald portal represents the story worlds, and the star represents discovery. Master asset: `assets/images/wikalino-icon.png` (1024 × 1024, opaque PNG). Mobile, splash, favicon, and PWA references are configured in app.json, app/+html.tsx, and public/manifest.webmanifest. Adaptive/maskable variants have extra padding for launcher cropping. Original assets remain available.

Native launcher and splash changes require a new app build; Expo Go keeps its own launcher icon. Publish a new PWA export to deliver the web icons; an existing home-screen installation may need reinstalling to refresh its icon.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished premium mobile app icon for Wikalino, a Filipino student reading adventure game. Square 1024x1024 opaque artwork, full bleed uniform deep forest green #123D32 background. One bold centered sculpted open storybook with warm ivory pages and golden edges, pages forming a subtle W silhouette. An upright luminous emerald magical portal rises from the book spine, with a single small golden star in its center representing discovery and learning. Charming high quality softly dimensional game emblem, crisp clean silhouette, restrained beautiful highlights, emerald and warm gold palette, minimal details legible at 48px. Entire book/portal emblem within central 65 percent of square with generous empty green margins. Front view, balanced symmetrical composition. No text, letters, watermark, border, rounded square container, scenery, people or mockup. This is the actual app icon asset.
